How to split a combined Flipkart label and invoice PDF

      
A combined Flipkart shipping document puts two separate documents on one A4 sheet: the shipping label on
        top and the tax invoice below. Flipkart Label Cropper works as an invoice separator and label cutter in one pass:
        each page becomes its own shipping label PDF and invoice crop, delivered as one combined PDF for the whole batch
        by default, or as individual _label.pdf and _invoice.pdf
        files when you want them.

      
    
        
  1. Add your files. Drag a batch of combined shipping-label PDFs onto the box above, or click it to
          browse. Multi-page files are fine — every page is split on its own.
    2. 
        
  2. Press “Process all”. The tool finds the Tax Invoice heading, measures where the printed content really is, and crops both sections to it.
    3. 
        
  3. Check the previews. Each file shows a thumbnail of both generated crops, so you can see the result before you download it.
    4. 
        
  4. Download. The whole batch arrives as one PDF — every label and its invoice, in order. Change the
          Output selector for labels only, invoices only, or a separate PDF per page you download one by one.
    5. 
        
  5. Print at 100%. Send the label PDF to a 4×6 thermal roll, or to A4 if you are printing on a laser or
          inkjet — at Actual size, never “fit to page”, so the barcode keeps its true module width.
    6. 
      

      
For the detail behind it — what the format contains and how the crop decides its edges —
        read the guide to splitting combined Flipkart shipping PDFs.

What is the invoice_labels PDF from Flipkart Seller Hub?

      
When you download dispatch documents for a batch of orders, the file you get is named
        invoice_labels_<number>.pdf, and the name says what is inside it. Every A4 page
        carries two documents: the shipping label on the top half, with the courier barcode and the delivery address,
        and the tax invoice on the bottom half, with the GSTIN, the invoice number and the HSN lines.

      
Both halves matter, but not to the same person at the same moment. The label has to go on the parcel and be
        scanned; the invoice has to be filed for accounting. So a mixed PDF like this is awkward to print as it arrives —
        printing it one way leaves invoice text wrapped around the label, and printing it the other way gives you labels you
        cannot file. Flipkart Label Cropper separates the two in a single pass, and treats a multi-page file as several orders: page 2
        becomes page 2 of both outputs instead of being lost.

      
The file is read inside this browser tab and is never uploaded — see
        why the PDFs never leave your device.

    


    

      
Auto Crop and Manual Crop

      

        

          

          
Auto Crop

          
The default. Flipkart Label Cropper reads the page text to locate the “Tax Invoice” heading, then renders the page and
            scans it to find where ink actually is, adding only a small uniform margin. Two levels of detection separate
            real content from page furniture, so colour bands, dashed perforation lines and page frames are left out and
            the crop wraps the printed block instead of the whole sheet. See
            how the crop works.

        

        

          

          
Manual Crop

          
For unusual layouts, different paper sizes or documents with more than two sections. Open the editor for a
            live page preview and drag the amber label and blue invoice regions, or press
            Snap to detected content and adjust from there. Regions are stored as percentages, so one set of boxes
            applies to every page and every file in the batch.

Frequently asked questions

      


      
What is Flipkart Label Cropper?

        
Flipkart Label Cropper is a free browser tool that takes a combined Flipkart shipping document — one A4 sheet holding both
          the shipping label and the tax invoice — and produces two separate, tightly cropped PDFs from it. It runs
          entirely in your browser, needs no account, and can process a whole batch of files in one go.


      
How do I split a combined Flipkart label and invoice PDF?

        
Drop your PDFs onto the box at the top of this page and press Process all. For every page the tool finds
          the “Tax Invoice” heading, measures where the printed content actually is, and crops each section to it.
          You get one combined PDF with every label and invoice in order. Multi-page files are numbered, so page 2 of
          order.pdf lands as order_p2 in the combined file; switch the
          Output selector to Individual PDFs and you get order_p2_label.pdf and so on,
          downloaded one by one or via your browser's download-all.


      
What is the difference between Auto Crop and Manual Crop?

        
Auto Crop is the default: it finds the split line from the “Tax Invoice” heading and trims each section
          to its printed content — text, rules, barcodes and the label's own border box — deliberately ignoring detached
          page furniture such as colour bands, dashed cut lines and page frames, so no blank paper comes along.
          Manual Crop opens a live preview with two draggable regions, amber for the label and blue for the
          invoice. Regions are stored as percentages, so they apply to every page and every file in the batch. Auto-tighten
          is on by default; turn it off to use your region exactly as drawn.


      
Can I process multiple PDF files at once?

        
Yes. Drop in 20 files or more; a few are processed at a time so the tab stays responsive, and you can stop a
          batch partway through. Multi-page files are handled page by page. If one file is corrupt or password-protected
          it is flagged inline and skipped, and the rest of the batch continues.


      
Are my uploaded PDF files sent to a server?

        
No. There is no upload step in the code. Your files are read with the browser's file API, and rendering,
          measuring, cropping and merging all happen in this tab — your document contents are never transmitted
          anywhere. The page does load its four PDF libraries from a public CDN, and once advertising is enabled the ad
          scripts load from Google, but nothing about your documents is sent with them. See the
          privacy policy.


      
Do I need to create an account?

        
No. There is no sign-up, no login and no email address required, and no payment details are ever requested.


      
Is Flipkart Label Cropper free to use?

        
Yes — free, with no file limit, no watermark and no subscription. The site is supported by the advertising
          shown on the page.


      
How do I print on 4x6 thermal paper without the invoice?

        
Set the Output selector to Labels only. Every page's shipping label is cropped content-tight and
          collected into one PDF — pages land at their cropped sizes, so a thermal printer scales the nearest one onto
          the roll. Pick Individual PDFs instead if your print dialog handles one label per file better.


      
What should I do if the automatic crop is incorrect?

        
Switch to Manual Crop, drag the label and invoice regions over the live preview, and apply them to the
          batch — they will be used for every page and every file. You can also raise the edge margin under
          Advanced settings. The activity log beneath the file list reports what the tool decided for each page,
          including when it fell back to a proportional split because no “Tax Invoice” heading was found. The
          guide covers this in more detail.


      
What size is a 4×6 shipping label in millimetres?

        
101.6 × 152.4 mm, which is why the roll is sold as “100 × 150 mm”. The other sizes in the same family
          are 4×5 in at 101.6 × 127 mm and 3×5 in at 76.2 × 127 mm. The full list, with what each size is used for, is in
          the label size table above.


      
Can I print four Flipkart labels on one A4 sheet?

        
Yes. Turn on 4 labels per A4 sheet in the toolbar above the file list: the label pages are packed four to a sheet on a 2×2
          grid, each one scaled to fit its quarter and centred, so nothing is cropped off or stretched. The A4 sheet is
          210 × 297 mm against the 4×6 thermal roll's 101.6 × 152.4 mm, so four labels fit with room for margins. Invoice
          pages are unaffected — they follow the sheets at their own cropped size.


      
Should I print the cropped label at 100%?

        
Yes — choose Actual size (100%) rather than “fit to page”. A uniform scale keeps the barcode module
          widths proportional, which is what a scanner reads; and because the crop hugs the printed label, there is no
          surrounding A4 to trim off first. Pick the media in the print dialog (4×6 for a thermal roll, A4 for a laser or
          inkjet).


      
Do I still need the tax invoice if I only print labels?

        
Yes. The invoice is the GST record — it carries the invoice number, GSTIN and HSN lines that your books and your
          accountant need. Use the Invoices option in the Output selector to collect every invoice into one PDF
          while you print the labels, so the two halves never have to be handled by hand.


      
Why is my Flipkart download called invoice_labels_….pdf?

        
Because the same download carries both documents — the shipping label and its tax invoice — one order to an A4
          page. The filename comes from the download itself; the two halves are exactly what this tool separates.


      
Does Flipkart Label Cropper work with Meesho or Amazon label PDFs?

        
Flipkart Label Cropper is built for, and tested against, the combined Flipkart shipping document. Documents from other
          marketplaces that put a shipping label and a tax invoice on one sheet under a “Tax Invoice” heading may split
          correctly, but that is not guaranteed — drop a single file in and check the thumbnails before running the rest
          of the batch. If the automatic split looks wrong, use Manual Crop.
