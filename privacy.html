Files you open in the tool are not uploaded. There is no upload endpoint, no cloud storage and no processing on our servers — because the service has no servers handling your documents at all. Splitting, measuring and cropping all happen inside your own browser tab.

1. Your PDF files

Flipkart Label Cropper is a client-side tool. When you drop PDF files onto the page, they are read using the browser's standard File and FileReader APIs. Every operation that follows — rendering the page with pdf.js, measuring the printed content, and building and combining the cropped output with pdf-lib — runs as JavaScript in your browser.

  • Your documents are never transmitted to us or to any third party by this tool.
  • Processed results exist only in your browser's memory until you choose to download them.
  • Nothing is written to persistent storage by the tool. Closing or reloading the tab discards everything.
  • We cannot see, retrieve, recover or delete your files, because we never receive them.

Because no personal data from your documents reaches us, we cannot use it, sell it, or disclose it to anyone. This is also why we can offer the tool without an account or a sign-in.

2. What we collect

There is no sign-up, no login and no payment. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or payment details, and we do not create user profiles.

Like most websites, the pages you visit are served by a hosting provider that may keep standard server logs for security and reliability. Those logs can include your IP address, the page requested, the referring page, your browser's user-agent string and a timestamp. We use them only to keep the site online and to investigate abuse; we do not use them to identify individuals.

This site itself sets no advertising cookies of its own. It stores two preferences in your browser's local storage — these are not cookies and are never sent to us:

Third-party advertising and its measurement partners may set their own cookies or similar identifiers. You control those through the banner and through your browser settings — see sections 4 and 5.

4. Advertising

This site is free to use and is funded by advertising. We use third-party advertising companies to serve ads when you visit the site.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising from many other vendors at aboutads.info or YourOnlineChoices.
  • For more information about how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see Google's partner sites notice.

Where required by law — for example for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland — we ask for your consent before any advertising cookie is set, and we pass your choice to our advertising partners using Google's Consent Mode. If you decline, ads may still be shown but they will not be personalised using cookie-based data. Advertising partners are contractually required to comply with applicable data protection law and Google's own EU user consent policy.

5. Your choices and controls

  • Change your consent choice at any time using the “Cookie settings” link in the footer of any page.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking advertising cookies does not affect the tool, which will keep working normally.
  • Use a browser that blocks trackers by default, or an advertising opt-out tool such as the ones linked above.
  • Use the tool offline. Save the page and open it locally; the document-splitting features work without contacting any ad server.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability — for example under the GDPR (EEA/UK) or the CCPA/CPRA (California). Because we do not collect personal data from your use of the tool and do not operate user accounts, we generally hold nothing about you to act on. Where personal data is processed for advertising, those requests should be directed to the relevant advertising vendor, and you can always exercise your rights through the controls in section 5.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your documents for advertising, profiling or training.

7. Children's privacy

This site is aimed at businesses and individuals handling shipping documents and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where that is the applicable age of digital consent). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an advertising interaction, please contact us and we will help you take it up with the relevant vendor.
